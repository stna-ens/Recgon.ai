<objective>
**Gap 1 (VERIFICATION phase_3_1_gaps#1):** The dispatcher currently assigns tasks even when EVERY candidate has zero fit signals. Live UAT 2026-05-15 confirmed.

**Gap 3 (user rule 2026-05-15):** "Recgon should NEVER EVER assign a task to someone because nobody else had time. If the task has nobody to do it because they are scheduled, it just should be assigned to a further time." Availability alone is never a reason to assign — qualified candidates being booked is a reason to *defer*, not a reason to dump on someone free.

**Fix — four-outcome decision tree in Pass 3:**

1. **Assign**: At least one candidate passes `hasMinimumFit` (skillOverlap | fitForKind | interestNudge ≥ SIGNAL_FLOOR=0.15) AND has current capacity (availabilityNow ≥ DEFER_FLOOR=0.3) AND Plan 03-05's grounded sentence is non-null → normal assignment.

2. **Triage — no clear fit** (`triage_note='no_clear_fit'`): NO candidate passes `hasMinimumFit`. The team lacks the skills/interest/kind-history for this task. Owner triages manually.

3. **Defer** (no triage_note, scheduledDate moved): Qualified candidates exist but ALL have availabilityNow < DEFER_FLOOR right now, AND task.priority < HIGH_PRIORITY_THRESHOLD=3. `findEarliestCapacityWindow` scans the next 4 weeks; if a window opens, the task's `scheduledDate` moves to that Monday and the task waits. Next dispatch run on that date tries again. The qualified candidates are not penalized for being busy now, and the task is not dumped on a less-qualified free teammate.

4. **Triage — no capacity** (`triage_note='no_capacity_in_window'`): Qualified candidates exist but NONE has capacity within the 4-week lookahead. The team is consistently slammed — owner needs to know.

4'. **Triage — high-priority bypass** (`triage_note='no_capacity_high_priority'`): Same situation as outcome 3 but `task.priority >= 3`. High-prio tasks can't sit waiting — straight to owner triage even if a future window exists.

**Coupling to Plan 03-05:** When 03-05's LLM cannot ground the Why-you sentence (returns null), the dispatcher treats that as a 5th triage path → `triage_note='no_grounded_reason'`. If the LLM can't say why this person fits, the assignment doesn't happen.

**Purpose:** Recgon stops assigning ghost tasks AND stops dumping work on whoever happens to be free. When the right people are busy, the task waits. When no one is right at all, the owner steps in.

**Output:**
- Pure helpers in `match.ts`: `hasMinimumFit`, `findEarliestCapacityWindow`, plus 4 named constants (SIGNAL_FLOOR, DEFER_FLOOR, DEFER_LOOKAHEAD_WEEKS, HIGH_PRIORITY_THRESHOLD).
- Triage + deferral paths wired in dispatcher's `runDispatch` (Pass 3) and `dispatchTask`.
- New `triage_note` column on `agent_tasks` (additive nullable).
- `markTaskForTriage`, `deferTaskScheduledDate`, `clearTriageNote` storage helpers.
- New return states `'triage'` + `'deferred'` join the dispatch result vocabulary.
- DispatchResult counts `triaged` + `deferred` separately so cron logs surface both decision branches.
</objective>
